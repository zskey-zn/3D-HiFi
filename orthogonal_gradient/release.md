HG002 and cotton T2T genomes were computationally fragmented into non-overlapping 1-, 2-, and 5-Mb segments. The intermediate orthogonal gradient testing data generated in this study are available via Figshare, where the dataset for HG002-mat can be accessed at https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33769771 and the corresponding data for cotton are accessible at https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33723265.
